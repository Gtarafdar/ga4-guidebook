# GA4 360 (Enterprise)

The paid tier of GA4, aimed at large organizations that need higher limits, service-level guarantees, and enterprise data-governance controls the free tier doesn't offer. Pricing is roughly six figures annually (commonly cited around $150K/year) and is negotiated directly with Google, not self-serve — treat any exact figure as one to reconfirm with a rep rather than quote as fixed.

## What it unlocks (vs. standard GA4)

| Capability | Standard | GA4 360 |
|---|---|---|
| Daily event volume | Lower ceiling before sampling/throttling | Extends to roughly 20 billion events/day |
| Data retention | 2 or 14 months | Extended options up to ~38-50 months |
| BigQuery export | Daily + streaming | Adds "fresh daily" mode |
| Subproperties | Not available | Available |
| Roll-up properties | Not available | Available |
| Support & SLA | Standard/community support | Dedicated support, contractual SLAs |

## Subproperties
Create restricted, filtered views of a single source property — for example, giving a regional team or an external partner access only to their slice of data, without duplicating tracking implementation.

*Business use:* "we need finance/legal/a partner to only see certain data" is a subproperty use case — it's an access/governance tool, not a way to get more tracking coverage.

## Roll-up properties
Combine data from multiple underlying properties into one aggregated view — for example, rolling up several regional or brand-specific properties into a single company-wide reporting property.

*Business use:* "can we see all three regional sites' data together in one place" is a roll-up property use case — it's the inverse of subproperties (combining rather than restricting).

## When it's actually worth it
GA4 360 is a real cost commitment, so it's worth reaching for specifically when a concrete limit is being hit — sampling/thresholding on a high-traffic property, needing subproperties/roll-ups for org structure, or needing an SLA/dedicated support for a business-critical analytics dependency — rather than as a general "better GA4" upgrade.

*Business use:* before recommending GA4 360, name the specific standard-tier limit that's actually the blocker (event volume, retention window, subproperty need, SLA requirement) — it makes the business case concrete instead of "enterprise sounds better."
