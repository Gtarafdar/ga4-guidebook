Read-aloud / Voice

Used by Play on Course and Resources lessons. Hear a short sample before you save — pick the voice that feels clearest to you.

Odd / novelty / robotic system voices are hidden. List shows clearer English options (Natural, Neural, Google, etc.).

Back to Course

Browser previews are free. ElevenLabs preview uses your key once per unique sample (then cached on this device). Saving is separate — preview never overwrites your saved choice until you click Save.

Open Coach
